# Documents and buying process

## Buying a property
1. Site visit and price negotiation.
2. Token payment (1%) to hold the property for 15 days.
3. Document verification: title deed, NOC from the society, and seller CNIC.
4. Transfer at the society office or the land record office, and full payment.
- Buyer needs: CNIC copy, 2 photographs, and proof of payment.
- Transfer fee and taxes are paid by the buyer and are about 4% to 6% of the property value.

## Buying a car
1. Test drive and inspection.
2. Price agreement and booking with a 10% advance.
3. Transfer at the Excise and Taxation office (Islamabad registration), usually same day.
- Buyer needs: CNIC original and copy, and 2 photographs. A company buyer needs an NTN letter.
- Every AutoHub car comes with a 200-point inspection report and a 6 month engine warranty.
