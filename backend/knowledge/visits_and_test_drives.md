# Site visits and test drives

## Visiting hours
- Site visits and test drives run Monday to Saturday. Slots: 10:00, 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00.
- We are closed on Sundays and on public holidays.
- Each slot is about 45 minutes. One customer per listing per slot.

## Test drive rules (AutoHub Motors)
- Bring your original CNIC and a valid driving licence.
- A sales officer rides along. Test drives follow a fixed 5 km route around the showroom.
- Customers must be 18 or older.

## Site visit rules (Skyline Realty)
- Our agent meets you at the property gate. The address is shared on WhatsApp 2 hours before the visit.
- Visits to occupied houses may need 24 hours notice.

## Rescheduling and cancellation
- You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 2 hours before the slot.
- To reschedule, just tell the assistant the new day and time.
