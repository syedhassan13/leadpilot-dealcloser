# Financing and payment plans

## Car financing (AutoHub Motors)
- Bank car financing is available through partner banks (Meezan, HBL, Bank Alfalah).
- Minimum down payment is 30% of the car price. Tenure is 1 to 5 years.
- Approval normally takes 7 to 10 working days after documents are complete.
- Salaried customers need 3 months of salary slips; business owners need 6 months of bank statements.
- AutoHub also accepts trade-ins: we inspect your current car for free and adjust its value against the new one.

## Property payment plans (Skyline Realty)
- Apartments in Gulberg Greens have an installment plan: 25% down payment and the rest over 3 years.
- Ready houses are sold on full payment or through bank home finance (Meezan Easy Home, HBL HomeLoan).
- Home finance usually needs 15% to 30% down payment and takes 3 to 6 weeks to approve.
- A token payment of 1% secures a property for 15 days while documents are checked.
