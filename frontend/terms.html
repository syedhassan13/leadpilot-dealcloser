Terms of Use

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Listings, prices and businesses are fictional demo data.

What LeadPilot is

LeadPilot is an AI assistant that answers questions about property and car listings and books site visits and test drives. Replies are written by an AI model and can be wrong. Prices are asking prices; the final price is agreed with a human sales officer at the visit.

Bookings

The assistant only books a visit after you confirm it. You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 2 hours before the slot, in the chat.

Fair use

  • Don't use the assistant for anything other than property or car inquiries.
  • Don't try to break, overload or misuse it, or access other people's data. Requests are rate-limited.
  • Don't share payment card numbers, passwords or OTP codes. We will never ask for them.

Pricing for dealers

Dealers subscribe monthly (Basic PKR 3,000 / month, Pro PKR 7,000 / month). Plans renew monthly and can be cancelled any time before the next billing date. Customers never pay to use LeadPilot.

Your data

See the Privacy Policy. You can delete your data any time from the chat.
