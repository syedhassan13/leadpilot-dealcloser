Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. LeadPilot is a student project (AI-4016, FAST Islamabad). Listings and businesses shown are fictional demo data.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your name and phone number (lead form), and the messages you send in the chat.
  • What you tell the assistant: budget, buying timeline, preferences (area, size, car model) and whether you need financing.
  • Bookings: the site visits or test drives you book.
  • Technical data: request counts per IP address for a short time, only to stop abuse. We use no analytics, ads or tracking cookies.

Please don't share card numbers, bank passwords, OTP codes or ID card numbers in the chat. If you do, card numbers are removed and ID card numbers are masked before the AI sees them.

Why we use it

To answer your questions, suggest matching properties or cars, book and manage your visits, send you follow-up messages a dealer has approved, and let the dealer you contacted see your inquiry.

Who it is shared with

  • The dealer you inquire with (Skyline Realty for property, AutoHub Motors for cars). Each dealer only sees its own leads.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): your chat messages and saved details are sent to the AI model to write replies.
  • Our hosting providers: Supabase (database), Render (server) and Vercel (website). They store and process data only to run the service.

We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

We keep your details and chat history while your inquiry is active and for at most 12 months after your last message, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Abuse-prevention counters expire within a day.

Your choices

  • Delete your data any time: open the chat and press 🗑 Delete my data, or use the link in the footer. This removes your chats, saved details, bookings and follow-ups from our database.
  • You can also ask the dealer to delete your data. They can do it from their dashboard.

Children

LeadPilot is for adults buying property or cars. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/syedhassan13/leadpilot-dealcloser/issues. To report a security problem privately, see security.txt.
