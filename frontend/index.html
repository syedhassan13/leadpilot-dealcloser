07

Questions dealers ask us.

Does LeadPilot replace my sales team?

No. It takes the first conversation off their plate: replying, qualifying and booking. Your people meet buyers who are ready, and agree the final price at the visit.

Will it make up prices or stock?

No. Every listing, price and slot comes from your inventory through a tool call. If something isn't in your data, it says so. A person always confirms the final price.

Can it book a visit by mistake?

Bookings need the buyer's explicit "yes" in their latest message, checked in code, not just by the AI. Fake confirmations injected into the chat are blocked.

How do I put it on my website?

Add one script tag and one <leadpilot-chat dealer="…"> element. The console's Settings page gives you the exact snippet.

What languages does it speak?

It's tested in English and understands everyday Roman Urdu confirmations such as "haan" and "theek hai".

What happens to my buyers' data?

It's stored in an access-locked database, used only to serve the conversation and your console, and never sold. Buyers can delete their data with one click. See our privacy policy.
